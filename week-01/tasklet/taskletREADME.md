# Proposal Generator — Daily Operator Workflow

An account manager uses the Proposal Generator to manage opportunities from initial drafting through the final outcome.

When a new opportunity arrives, the operator creates or reviews the proposal in the Drafting stage. Reusable pricing, scope, and terms blocks reduce repetitive data entry and help keep proposals consistent.

Once the draft is complete, the proposal moves to Internal Review. The operator checks the proposal and obtains approval before it can be sent to the prospect. This prevents unapproved proposals from being sent accidentally.

After approval, the proposal moves to Sent. The operator can monitor engagement such as opens and clicks and use those signals to determine when follow-up is needed.

If a prospect engages with the proposal but does not move forward, a follow-up task can be created so the account manager knows what action to take.

Finally, completed opportunities are moved to Won-Lost, giving the agency a clear view of proposal outcomes and the entire proposal lifecycle.
